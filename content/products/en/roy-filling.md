**羝 (dī) ICP Filing** is a content provider registration service established by Solsynth under the authorization of the Republic of Yang's Network Information Office. It allows citizens of the Republic of Yang and other countries to browse websites certified by Solsynth and its partners, improving content quality on the internet and supporting the growth of local internet enterprises in the Republic of Yang.

## What is 羝 ICP Filing?

羝 ICP Filing is a community-driven friendly link collection and certification platform. Any citizen or enterprise of the Republic of Yang can submit their website. After review, the website receives a 羝 ICP filing number and is listed in the public filing directory.

The filing directory is more than just a web navigation — it represents an endorsement of internet content quality by the Republic of Yang's Network Information Office. Certified websites display the 羝 ICP badge, symbolizing their trusted identity in the Republic of Yang's cyberspace.

## Key Features

- **Website Filing Application**: Submit your website information for manual or automated review
- **Filing Directory Search**: Browse registered websites by category or keyword
- **羝 ICP Badge**: Obtain an exclusive badge after approval to display on your website footer
- **Open API**: Developers can query filing data via API, see [Suki](https://kb.solsynth.dev/republic-of-yang/icp-filling/) for details

## Filing Requirements

To obtain 羝 ICP Filing, your website must meet the following basic requirements:

1. The website content must be legal and compliant, free of malware, fraud, or other illegal information
2. The website must be accessible with reasonable availability
3. The website owner must provide verifiable contact information
4. Compliance with the regulations of the Republic of Yang's Network Information Office

## How to Use

Submit your website information on the [羝 ICP Filing page](https://solsynth.dev/icp). Once approved, you will receive a filing number. You can also search and browse registered websites on the same page.

## Domain Challenge

To verify your ownership of the domain, you need to complete a challenge via DNS TXT record:

1. If you have a **Solarpass** account, add a TXT record at `_roy_challenge.<your domain>` with the value being your Solarpass username (**including the @ symbol**)
2. If you don't have a Solarpass account yet, email **lily@solsynth.dev** first to request a challenge string, then add it to the TXT record at `_roy_challenge.<your domain>`

Once added, the system will automatically verify and approve your filing.

> **Note:** The system verification may have a delay. If the system attempts three times (each with a 24-hour interval) and still cannot find the correct TXT record, we will reject your request, and you will need to manually submit again.

## Submitting via Email

If you prefer not to use the online form, you can also submit your application via email.

Send an email to **lily@solsynth.dev** with the subject **ROY 备案申请** and include the following information:

- **Site Name**: The name of your website
- **Site URL**: The full URL of your website
- **Domain**: Your domain name
- **Contact**: Email, Discord, or other way we can reach you
- **Notes (optional)**: Any additional information you'd like to include

**Example:**

```
To: lily@solsynth.dev
Subject: ROY 备案申请

Site Name: Solsynth
Site URL: https://solsynth.dev
Domain: solsynth.dev
Contact: lily@solsynth.dev
Notes: Official website of Solsynth
```

We will review your application and contact you within 7 working days.
